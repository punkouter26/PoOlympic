Spectacle features — UI changes

Fall recovery, slalom tactics, real weather, AI commentator, haptics. Captured in the editor at 1440 × 3088 (shown at half size), Event 19 Terminal Velocity, line-up MATT / ZOMBIE / GRANDMA / KIM × 2. Red boxes mark what changed.

Settings › Game

1 Two new rows. Real weather (wind, rain), off by default: asks Open-Meteo for today's host city once per session; wind pushes the athletes, rain makes the track slippery. AI commentator, off by default: reads "No key" until an Anthropic API key is found (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY, or anthropic_api_key.txt in the app's data folder).

Settings › Display

2 New row Vibrate on impacts, on by default: the phone buzzes on body slams and cube hits, longer and stronger with the contact force. Nothing happens on desktop.

Standings row of a fallen runner

3 Before, this row read "FELL" and the runner was out. A runner with a get-up brain (KIM today) now reads DOWN · getting up, stays in the race, and her result ends with "· got up". Here K8 was shoved over by hand (6 m/s) to show it; she got up and finished.
