Follow-up: camera on the current winner, simpler stats card

User feedback on the live heats: too many cuts, the camera should show the current winner close enough to see the face (whole body in the picture), never shoot from inside the stands, and the stats card flickered.

  • A → 1 Live pacing: a front close-up of the current winner (3.2 m, 42° lens: head to feet, face visible) for ~8 s, then one context shot (trackside / head-on / high-wide; arenas: the slow orbit) for 5 s. No shot under 4 s, no tension-driven fast cutting; the close-up keeps its athlete for the whole shot. A near fall (danger ≥ 0.7, 8 s apart) still cuts to that athlete. In a tie (last one standing) the "winner" is the steadiest athlete over ~3 s, held ≥ 5 s. Every camera is clamped inside the bowl (never past the track's outer edge into the crowd).
  • B → 2 Stats card: speed, power, confidence only; refreshed once a second; it shows the athlete the camera is on.
  • 3 The tag says why that athlete is on screen: LEADER, IN TROUBLE (near fall) or WINNER.
