UI consolidation: before vs after

Portrait "top 10" (29 Sep 2026): one viewport and no scrolling, the result shown inside the standings, one shared 5-slot anchor frame (top-left title · top-centre FPS · top-right menu · bottom-left debug · bottom-right version), shared size tokens, and a layout test at 4 phone sizes. Portrait Game view 960 × 2658 in the editor (narrower than any real phone, so the tightest case). A dashed orange = the problem before, 1 red = what changed.

Main menu

  • 1 New anchor bar: title with the lineup status under it (this was E, the status line under PLAY), the FPS pill, and the ☰ menu.
  • A B → 2 The roster cards overlapped the LANES heading, and there was a separate "Fill all 8" button with a hint. Now one card per athlete: tap it to put that athlete in all 8 lanes (×8).
  • C → 3 Lanes 7–8 ran into the EVENT row. Now a 4 × 2 grid: tap a lane to switch its athlete. The LANES and ROSTER headings are gone.
  • D → 4 The event ScrollView is now a fixed 3-column grid that grows to fill the screen, so all 11 events are visible with no scrolling.
  • 5 Detail panel with a fixed height: name, brain badge, rules and world record for the selected event.
  • 6 One action row: ★ records · GAUNTLET · PLAY (all 104 px tall).
  • 7 Bottom bar: debug (bottom-left), the last gauntlet result, and the version (bottom-right).

World records: a separate screen → a mode of the menu

  • F → 8 9 The records board is no longer a separate screen. With ★ on, every event tile shows its world record and the detail panel shows the selected event's all-time top 5.
  • 10 Gauntlet: "GAUNTLET: OFF" is now a chip that lights up with a count, the tiles show their order badges, and PLAY shows how many events.

Race, live (Event 19 Terminal Velocity)

  • 11 12 13 Top-left: title with a one-line subtitle ("Event 19" becomes "E19"). Top-centre: FPS. Top-right: the clock and info line next to the ☰ menu. The title block and clock box used to be two separate panels.
  • G → 14 The column header row (# LANE RESULT CONF) is gone. The expand hint ("8 ▼") sits in the last row.
  • H → 15 The 3-tile stats card (about 150 px) is now one 64 px story strip: chip · lane · LEADER / IN TROUBLE / WINNER · speed · power · confidence and sparkline.
  • I J → 16 The ticker box and the New heat / Menu buttons are merged into the bottom bar: debug (bottom-left), 2-line ticker, version (bottom-right). New heat and Main menu moved into the ☰ sheet.
  • The camera gap (where the game is visible) is about 17 % taller: in these shots it grows from 1362 to 1590 of 2000 px (68 % → 80 % of the screen).

Race, result

  • K → 17 18 The result card that covered the game is gone. The standings become the result: gold / silver / bronze rows, a WR badge on a record-setting winner, one world-record line (mark · holder · "#5 ALL-TIME" / "NEW RECORD!" pulse), and the heat bests as badges (TOP / PWR / CLOSE).
  • 19 The primary action appears in the bottom bar (New heat, or Next event in a gauntlet), 104 px tall.
  • 20 The winner banner shows for 2.5 s and then clears, so the podium and winner shots stay clear.
