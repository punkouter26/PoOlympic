App audit: UI before vs after

11 Oct 2026, Event 19 Terminal Velocity and the main menu in the editor at 1440 × 3088. Red boxes mark what is new. The "before" pictures are the last captures of the same screens (heats differ, layouts are the same).

1. Live heat: status bars and camera lock

  • 1 Two bars per athlete, no text: stability on top (green and full = steady; it drops as the athlete gets close to falling), effort below (turns from cyan to red as the athlete tires). Hidden for athletes who are out and at the result.
  • 2 Tap a row to lock the camera on that athlete (cyan row). Drag the picture to orbit around them; tap the row again to release. The "8 ▼" corner still opens all 8 rows.
  • The story strip at the bottom adds "tired NN%" once an athlete is at least 20 % tired.

2. Settings: one tabbed sheet at the bottom

  • 3 There was no settings screen before (the menu only had "Performance: show / hide"). Menu ▸ Settings opens this sheet: Audio (four volumes), Display (camera shake, zoom on trouble, focus blur, impact marks), Game (betting slip, debug readout, action log, erase save). Each value is one tap target that steps or toggles; nothing scrolls. The menu of an event also has "Speed" and, after a heat, "Replay the finish".

3. Match card: Replay

  • 4 Replay shows the last 5 seconds of the heat at half speed, then the card comes back. Tap the picture to skip.

4. Debug readout (TAB)

  • 5 TAB now toggles it (F3, a three-finger tap and the bottom-left button still work). Before it was one line of frame timings. It now adds three lines about the athlete on camera: which brain and what it costs, balance, effort and tiredness, step rate, joints over the 18 rad/s bar, sliding and contacts.
