Balance lines removed and the single-event match card: before vs after

1 Oct 2026, Event 1 The Iron Pedestal in the editor at 1440 × 3088. Red boxes mark what changed; dashed orange boxes mark the old state.

1. The green balance lines are gone

  • A Each athlete had a coloured outline around the feet, a ring under the centre of mass and a vertical line up to the body (green, amber or red by balance).
  • 1 The effect is removed from all 14 event scenes, for every athlete. The HUD's confidence column and the "IN TROUBLE" tag still show who is close to falling.

2. A single event now ends: heat, medal ceremony, match card, main menu

Before, an event started from the main menu ran heat after heat (the ticker in the old capture reads "Heat 2 — athletes to their marks") and the ceremony was cut off when the next heat began. There is no "before" capture of the card: it is a new screen.

  • 2 The camera stays on the podium for the whole ceremony (about 8 s after the result). The next heat is held at the start, so nothing restarts behind it.
  • 3 The button under the game reads "Results" during the ceremony: it skips straight to the card. Before, it read "New heat".
  • 4 The match card: event, heat number, all 8 places with their marks (gold, silver and bronze rows), the world record, and the heat bests.
  • 5 OK goes back to the main menu.
  • 6 The clock, the standings and the ticker keep the finished heat while the card is up.

Gauntlets and demo mode keep their own flow (Next event / the countdown); their ceremony now also runs to the end while the stage waits.
