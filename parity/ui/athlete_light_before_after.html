Brighter athletes: before vs after

2 Oct 2026, Event 5 The Gust Gauntlet, winner shot, in the editor at 1440 × 3088. Both pictures are the same frozen frame with the new lights switched off and on. Red boxes mark what changed; dashed orange boxes mark the old state.

  • A The only direct light on the athletes was the key light, which points almost straight down, so heads and shoulders were lit and everything facing the camera was dark. The baked light around the track is dim too (a third of the measuring points are close to black).
  • 1 Two soft lights without shadows now shine on the athletes from opposite sides, at 30° above the horizon (strength 0.9 and 0.6). They are in every one of the 14 event scenes as the object AthleteFill, so they can be tuned by hand in the scene.
  • B 2 The stadium, the crowd, the track and the platform are unchanged: the new lights are set to light only the athletes and the podium statues. Nothing had to be rebaked.
