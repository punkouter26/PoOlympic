Demo mode: before vs after

Demo (attract) mode, 30 Sep 2026: after 45 s without input the main menu starts an endless gauntlet of all 11 playable events with random lineups. Results advance by themselves, a season table builds up across series, and any tap returns to the menu. Same event and moment (Event 22 Emergency Brake, result) in normal play and in demo mode; Device Simulator, 1440 × 3088. A dashed orange = before, 1 red = what changed.

Result (Event 22 Emergency Brake)

  • A Title chip: the event subtitle only.
  • B New heat waits for a tap; in a gauntlet it reads Next event and also waits.
  • 1 NEW caption line DEMO · tap to exit · <lineup theme> (All MATT, Mixed lineup, 4 v 4, One ZOMBIE among 7 MATT).
  • 2 Gauntlet standing after this event (existing), plus the NEW season line: series played, series won per body, points per event per body (fair across lineups with different counts). Saved in PlayerPrefs, so it survives restarts.
  • 3 The button becomes a countdown, Next event in 6 … 1 (New series in … after the last event), and moves on by itself 6 s after the result. It is not tappable in demo: any tap anywhere exits to the menu.
