3 · Effort

Episode_Reward/torques (penalty on joint torque, scaled so equal effort relative to each body's strength costs the same). Upper pink line (−0.155) is GRANDMA; lower pink line (−0.204) is ZOMBIE; dark line (−0.049) is MATT.

🧸 Toddler

Standing is harder work for Grandma than for Matt. She has to push more.

🎒 Child

This line shows how hard the robot's muscles work. Closer to zero means less work. Matt barely has to try. Grandma works about three times as hard for her strength, because she is weaker and leans forward a little. Zombie works hardest of all.

🎓 Adult

The penalty weight is divided by the body's torque scale squared, so the term measures effort relative to capacity. GRANDMA settles at −0.155, about three times MATT's −0.049 and below ZOMBIE's −0.204. That fits a 60 % strength body holding a 10° stoop with a forward chest and head: 24 Nm at the waist in a quiet stand. All three show the same shape: effort spikes while the policy learns to stay up (iterations 100–250), then falls as it finds a cheaper stance.

What is not established

Two body changes were made before the real cause of the failed first run (one environment) was found, and were kept without a controlled comparison: servo stiffness not scaled by her strength factor, and the stance moved back (hips 17°, ankles 7°). Her mass is 65 kg, chosen from the stocky mesh; the height question quoted about 60 kg. She is not in Unity or the phone build yet.
