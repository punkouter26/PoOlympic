KIM Rung 1 training review: walking and running forward

2026-10-11 00:46, run k1_v1, iteration 1999 · KIM (1.55 m, 54 kg, 90 % strength) · 2000 iterations in 71 min, 4096 environments, warm start from her Rung 0 brain (k0_v1 it 1499). Earlier runs of the same task (GRANDMA g1_v1, MATT r1_v2, ZOMBIE z1_v1) are shown from their saved charts: this clone of the repository no longer has their TensorBoard logs.

Verdict: KIM passes Rung 1, 10 out of 10

In one sentence: she covers the 30 m test at every commanded speed with no falls and inside her lane on all ten seeds, at iterations 1000, 1500 and 1999.

Compared with the two bodies trained on this scaled recipe (MATT's gate history was not looked up): GRANDMA's best Rung 1 checkpoint passed 8/10 and ZOMBIE's 3/10. KIM follows the commanded speed more closely than GRANDMA did at the same point (track_lin 1.84 against 1.67 early on, 1.63 against 1.58 at the end) and her steering error ends lower (0.73 against 1.05 rad/s). She was also the only one that started from a standing brain instead of from scratch, so the early part of the curves is not a like-for-like comparison.

1 · Speed tracking

Episode_Reward/track_lin (how closely the commanded forward speed is followed). The drops are the curriculum raising the top speed (0.92 → 1.84 → 2.76 m/s for KIM). Top: KIM. Bottom: GRANDMA, MATT and ZOMBIE.

🧸 Toddler

Kim is learning to walk as fast as she is told. Each time we ask her to go faster the line drops a little, then she catches up.

🎒 Child

The line is high when the robot moves at exactly the speed it was asked for. The sudden drops are where we started asking for faster running. Kim already knew how to stand, so she picked up slow walking very quickly, and she ends a little higher than Grandma did.

🎓 Adult

1.53 by iteration 100 and 1.81 by 250 (GRANDMA 1.67 at 250 from scratch), 1.84 at 500, then 1.73 at 1000 and 1.59 at 1500 after the two curriculum steps, ending at 1.63 with a top command of 2.79 m/s (GRANDMA 1.58, MATT 1.60, ZOMBIE 1.57 at the same point). The ten test seeds drew commands up to 1.87 m/s (the test's range ends at 2.76), so they did not probe the top of her envelope; Rung 2's gate does.

2 · Steering error

Metrics/athlete/error_vel_yaw (turn-rate error in rad/s; lower is better). Top: KIM. Bottom: the earlier runs.

🧸 Toddler

When Kim runs fast she wobbles a little. She wobbles less than Grandma.

🎒 Child

This line shows how much the robot turns when it should go straight. Lower is better. It is low while Kim walks slowly and climbs when we ask for fast running, but it stops at about 0.7. Grandma's and Zombie's lines kept climbing past 1.0. That is why Kim stays in her lane in the test.

🎓 Adult

0.44 rad/s at iteration 500, 0.55 at 1000, 0.73 at 1500 and 1999: level with MATT's 0.74–0.78 and below GRANDMA's 1.05 and ZOMBIE's 1.13 at iteration 2000. The gates agree in direction: worst lane drift grows from 0.19 m (it 1000) to 0.32 m (it 1999) but stays inside the bar on every seed. The metric includes the roll and pitch rates of the gait, so part of the rise is body rocking at speed.

3 · Staying on her feet

Train/mean_episode_length (1000 = stayed up for the whole 20 s episode). Top: KIM. Bottom: the earlier runs.

🧸 Toddler

Kim almost never falls down, even when she runs.

🎒 Child

At the top of the chart the robot never falls. Kim starts near the top, because she already knew how to stand. When we ask for faster running she trips for a moment, then she is fine again.

🎓 Adult

965 of 1000 at iteration 50 (warm start), 1000 from iteration 250, 998 at the end with a 2.79 m/s top command. The fall penalty term is −0.002 at the end. The two falls at the iteration 500 gate are from before the first curriculum step and are gone at every later gate.

What is not established

Whether her "quick short steps" style shows: the swing-time reward was capped at 0.45 s × √λ instead of 0.6, and her mean swing time ends at 0.18 s, but GRANDMA's and MATT's swing times were not measured for comparison, so the cap may not be what set it. Speeds above 1.87 m/s are trained (to 2.79 m/s) but were not drawn by these ten test seeds. No render of her running: the MuJoCo viewer was not opened during the run.
