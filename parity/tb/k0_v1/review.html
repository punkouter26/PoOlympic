KIM Rung 0 training review: standing and shove recovery

2026-10-10 23:35, run k0_v1, iteration 1499 · KIM (1.55 m, 54 kg, 90 % strength) · 1500 iterations in 53 min, 4096 environments, from scratch. Earlier runs of the same task (GRANDMA g0_v2, ZOMBIE z0_v1, MATT r0_v2) are shown from their saved charts: this clone of the repository no longer has their TensorBoard logs, so the lines cannot be drawn on one chart.

Verdict: KIM passes Rung 0

In one sentence: she stands upright, recovers from every shove and dropped cube without falling, and passes the standing test 10 out of 10 at iterations 1000 and 1499; in the harder 30-seed check she passes 27.

Compared with the earlier bodies on the same task: better than GRANDMA and ZOMBIE on the 30-seed check (27 against 24 and 25), just below MATT (29). She learned to stay up a little slower than GRANDMA (iteration 177 against 119) and a little faster than ZOMBIE and MATT (214, 210). She stands with the least effort for her strength of the three trained from scratch.

2 · Total reward

Train/mean_reward. Top: KIM (ends at 82). Bottom: ZOMBIE ends at 97, GRANDMA at 88, MATT at 82.

🧸 Toddler

The robot gets points for standing nicely. Kim's points go up and then stay high.

🎒 Child

This is the robot's score for each try. Kim's score climbs fast for 500 rounds, then slowly, and levels off at about 82. That is the same score as Matt, because the two are scored in the same way. Grandma and Zombie get extra points for their own styles, so their lines end higher without being better.

🎓 Adult

KIM is at 76.6 by iteration 500, 81.7 at 1000 and 82.5 at 1499: a plateau from about iteration 900, and the 1000 and 1499 checkpoints gate the same. Her reward terms are MATT's (upright term, no style terms), which is why she ends at his level; GRANDMA's and ZOMBIE's totals include style terms and are not comparable. Her posture terms are near their ceilings (height 0.999, upright 0.992).

3 · Effort

Episode_Reward/torques (penalty on joint torque, scaled so equal effort relative to each body's strength costs the same). Top: KIM (ends at −0.046). Bottom: GRANDMA −0.155, ZOMBIE −0.204, MATT −0.049.

🧸 Toddler

Standing is easy for Kim. She does not have to push hard.

🎒 Child

This line shows how hard the robot's muscles work. Closer to zero means less work. At first Kim works very hard to stay up. Then she finds an easier way to stand, and the line climbs back towards zero. She ends up working about as little as Matt, and three times less than Grandma.

🎓 Adult

Same shape as the other bodies: effort peaks while the policy learns to stay up (−0.26 at iteration 200), then falls as it finds a cheaper stance, to −0.046 at the end, level with MATT's −0.049 and about a third of GRANDMA's −0.155. That fits a light, upright body with 90 % of its size-scaled torque: nothing to hold against gravity except small corrections.

What is not established

Her height, mass and strength were chosen by Claude from the scan ("give her unique characteristics"), not measured. The rig's hip joints sit at 0.44 of her height, which gives her short legs; whether that is the person's real proportion or the auto-rig's placement was not checked. This page has no render of her standing: the MuJoCo viewer was not opened during the run. She is not in Unity yet.
