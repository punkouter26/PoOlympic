Verdict: KIM passes Rung 2, 10 out of 10

In one sentence: she follows speed, turn, side-step and backward commands inside the bars, spins 360° in 2.3–2.5 s, brakes from a run and never falls, on all ten seeds at every checkpoint of the last run; on 30 other seeds the chosen checkpoint passes 29.

1 · Turn-rate error

Metrics/athlete/error_vel_yaw (how far her turn rate is from the commanded one during practice, rad/s; lower is better). Top: KIM's three runs. Bottom: GRANDMA's g2_v7.

🧸 Toddler

Lower is better. Pink goes up, which is bad. Orange and purple go down, which is good.

🎒 Child

This is how much Kim turns wrongly. In the pink run we kept asking for sharper and sharper turns (the two jumps), and her mistakes grew. In the orange run a new trick was used: every move is also practised as its mirror image, left for right. Her mistakes shrank steadily. Purple carried on from there and is the lowest of all.

🎓 Adult

k2_v1 falls to 0.87 rad/s within its first command stage, then steps up at iterations ~520 and ~1000 as the curriculum widens the turn range to ±2.7 rad/s, ending at 1.67. Part of that rise is simply larger commands, so the level is not comparable across stages. k2_v2 starts on the full range with the lateral-acceleration cap, the mirror loss and command bands, and falls from 1.25 to 0.88; k2_v3 from 0.88 to 0.76 and still falling slowly. The gate saw the same thing from the other side: in k2_v1 one turntable direction was 0.3–0.5 s slower than the other and only right-hand side-steps failed; from k2_v2 on, the two directions are within 0.2 s.

2 · Total reward

Train/mean_reward. Top: KIM's three runs. Bottom: GRANDMA's g2_v7 (purple) and g2_v8 (yellow).

🧸 Toddler

More points is better. Purple has the most points.

🎒 Child

This is Kim's score for each try. The pink line drops twice: that is not her getting worse, it is the exercises getting harder. The orange line climbs the whole time, and the purple line starts where orange stopped and climbs a little more.

🎓 Adult

k2_v1 reaches 111 on the first stage, then drops to ~91 and ~85 at the two curriculum steps and recovers only to 88: on the full command range its recipe had stopped improving, with the action noise rising from 0.42 to 0.53. k2_v2 (entropy 0.001, start noise capped at 0.3) climbs from 95 to 108 without levelling off; k2_v3 from 109 to 113, nearly flat after iteration 600, which fits its gate being 10/10 from iteration 200 on. The three runs do not share one reward definition (k2_v2 and k2_v3 use a sharper speed kernel with a lower weight), so only the shapes compare.

3 · Speed tracking

Episode_Reward/track_lin (points for moving at the commanded speed). Top: KIM's three runs. Bottom: GRANDMA's g2_v7 and g2_v8.

🧸 Toddler

Kim moves at the speed she is told. Orange and purple end a little higher than they start.

🎒 Child

The line is high when Kim moves at exactly the speed she was asked for. The pink line sits higher only because it is scored more gently. For orange and purple the scoring is strict: a small mistake costs a lot. They still climb.

🎓 Adult

k2_v1's term has weight 3 and a 0.5 m/s kernel (1.49 on the first stage, 1.28 at the end); k2_v2 and k2_v3 use weight 2 and a 0.28 m/s kernel, so their 0.89 → 0.95 → 1.0 of 2.0 is on a stricter scale. GRANDMA on the same strict kernel ended g2_v8 at 0.97–0.99 with a top speed capped at 2.8 m/s; KIM reaches the same level on her full envelope (3.67 m/s). The last segments over the gate's bar were fast side-steps (0.76–0.87 m/s); k2_v3 gave those 30 % of the commands and they passed at its first gate.

What is not established

Event heats with this brain (Slalom, Crab, Gust Gauntlet and the track races) have not been run; GRANDMA's gate-best brain fell in the Slalom, so the gate alone does not settle the choice between iterations 200, 600 and 999. The one 30-seed miss, a sprint at 3.45 m/s with a turn, is at the top of an envelope that was scaled by her height; her legs are short for her height, so the bars are slightly harder for her than for a body of average proportions. Whether k2_v3's side-step emphasis was needed, or k2_v2 would have reached 10/10 with more iterations, was not tested.
