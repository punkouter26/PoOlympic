Rung S training review: rs_v6

2026-09-30 21:17, iteration 3299 · MATT (mattbio body) · 1000 iterations in 50 min · rs_v5c = previous run (1549 → 2548) rs_v6 = this run (from rs_v5c 2300 → 3299)

Verdict: better at marching, worse at walking and turning; flamingo was learned and then lost

In one sentence: the march fix worked (every test athlete now lifts its knees to 83–97 % of the called height, where five of ten did not lift at all at the starting checkpoint), but the athlete wanders off its spot while marching, the one-leg stance was solid at iteration 2550 and gone by 2800, and walking and turning got worse even though they were given more practice.

What changed in rs_v6: (1) walking practice 20 % → 35 % of the environments, (2) march reward only for the leg that should be in the air, (3) a penalty when the standing foot slides during the one-leg stance.

How to read the charts: each skill got 13 % of the environments in rs_v6 instead of 16 %, so the same performance scores about 19 % lower on a skill chart. A cyan line slightly below the dark one can mean "equal".

🎓 Adult

rs_v6 ends at 0.084 (smoothed) against rs_v5c's 0.076, with 13 % march environments instead of 16 %. Corrected for that share it is about 0.108 against 0.076, roughly 40 % higher, and still rising at the end with no plateau. The swing-leg-only progress term removed the pay for standing still near the profile's zero crossings. Drill at 3299: mean peak lift 0.16–0.24 m for calls of 0.17–0.29 m. Of the six failures, five are pelvis drift of 0.43–0.90 m (all at cadences of 1.3 Hz and above) and one is rhythm 6 % fast. Nothing rewards staying on the spot while marching.

2 · Flamingo: learned by 2550, lost again as march was learned

Episode_Reward/skill_flamingo (lifted foot off the ground and 10 cm up, standing foot planted and still)

🧸 Toddler

The robot tries to stand on one leg like a flamingo. The line is not going up. It still wobbles.

🎒 Child

This score is high when one foot stays in the air and the other foot does not slide. Early in this try the robot could do it perfectly, ten times out of ten. Then it learned to march, and it forgot how to stand on one leg: by the end it hops around again. The line slowly sinking shows that forgetting.

🎓 Adult

Share-corrected, rs_v6 ends near 0.072–0.083 against rs_v5c's 0.089–0.093: slightly worse, and trending down inside the run. The new slip penalty term stays at about −0.048 for the whole run, so the policy is not reducing stance-foot speed. The original drill drew no random command, so its ten seeds were one trial. It was changed afterwards (extra settle of 0–1 s, random foot order) and two checkpoints were re-tested: 2550 passes 10/10 (0 touches, stance slip 0.5–2.2 cm) and 3299 fails 0/10 (65–85 touch ticks, 14–18 cm slip). So the skill was genuinely there at 2550 and was lost between 2550 and 2800, the same window in which march went from 0 to 2/10. 2800 and 3050 were not re-tested (single-trial fails).

3 · Turning accuracy keeps eroding

Episode_Reward/track_ang (how closely the commanded turn rate is followed; paid in every mode)

🧸 Toddler

The robot is forgetting how to turn nicely. The line goes down. That is not good.

🎒 Child

This score is high when the robot turns exactly as fast as it is told. It was already slowly going down in the dark try. In the light-blue try it goes down faster, even though we let the robot practise walking more often. Learning the new tricks is making it forget an old one.

🎓 Adult

rs_v6 begins where rs_v5c left off (about 0.99, so the larger walking share did not shift the term by itself) and declines almost linearly to 0.83, about 17 % in 1000 iterations and faster than rs_v5c. track_lin falls too (1.23 → 1.18). The Rung 2 gate agrees: 4–6/10 against 8/10 at the starting checkpoint, with no falls. Raising the walking share to 35 % did not protect locomotion. The cause is not established; candidates are interference from in-place marching (it drives the same gait clock at zero velocity) and the walking-away habit seen in the march drill. Next run: penalise pelvis drift in march mode, and check whether yaw tracking recovers when march is trained with a spot-keeping term.
