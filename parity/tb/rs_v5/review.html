Rung S training review: joint-space leg guides

2026-09-30 15:15, iteration 1549 · MATT (mattbio body) · rs_v4 = previous recipe (950) rs_v5 (950 → 1068, died with its session) rs_v5b = same recipe resumed (1050 → 1549) rs_v5c = continuing now (→ 2548)

Verdict: rs_v5 is doing better than rs_v4, but no leg skill passes yet

In one sentence: telling the robot which way to bend its hips, knees and ankles got the squat and the march moving for the first time, but it still squats about 9 cm too shallow, lifts its knees about 6 cm instead of 15–30 cm, and standing on one leg hasn't improved.

Why: rs_v1–v4 only rewarded the result (pelvis height, knee height). A robot standing straight was too far from any payoff to feel which way to move. rs_v5 adds two rewards on the joint angles of a plausible squat, march or flamingo pose, so every small bend toward the pose now pays. In the training rewards, squat and march lift climb steadily through rs_v5b, where rs_v4 went flat. Flamingo was already close to its drill and doesn't benefit. In the drills, torso aim fell from 7/10 to 4/10 and running from 9/10 to 8/10 while the legs improved, so the new skills are competing a little with the old ones.

🎓 Adult

rs_v4 was flat at ~0.017. rs_v5b reaches ~0.031, and the slope flattens after ~1450. In the drill (1.3 Hz, 0.25 m command), measured knee rise went 1.8 → 3.7 → 5.2 → 6.0 cm across the gates. A known weakness: skill_leg_progress normalises by the default pose's error, floored at 0.1 rad. Near the profile's zero crossings the target is almost the standing pose, so standing still still earns part of the march guide. If rs_v5c plateaus, the fix is to pay march progress only on the swing leg while its profile is up, and/or a lift-height curriculum (start at 5–10 cm).

3 · Flamingo: flat in every run

Episode_Reward/skill_flamingo (lifted foot ≥ 10 cm up, stance foot planted, × slip gate)

Standing on one leg like a flamingo. All the lines are the same height, so it isn't getting better.

🎒 Child

The robot already knows how to lift a foot, but its standing foot still slides and its lifted foot sometimes taps the floor. The new knee-bending hint didn't help this trick, because the robot was already bending the knee. What it needs is better balance, not a different pose.

🎓 Adult

~0.10 (≈ 2/3 of its ceiling) in rs_v4, v5 and v5b with no trend. The drill bounced: touches 154 → 51 → 14 → 50, slip 0.18 → 0.095 → 0.165 m. Every seed gives nearly identical flamingo numbers (the drill uses a fixed command per seed), so each gate is effectively one sample and checkpoint-to-checkpoint noise is large. The bottleneck is stance-foot stability. The next candidate is an explicit stance-foot velocity penalty or a longer hold, not more pose guidance.
