Verdict: marching is fixed (9 of 10); flamingo, reach and walking are not

In one sentence: rewarding the athlete for staying on its spot turned the march from 4 of 10 into 9 of 10, with full knee lift and 6–14 cm of drift instead of up to 90 cm, and squats stay perfect; standing on one leg still fails every trial, reaching did not move, and walking and turning stay at 6 of 10.

What changed in rs_v7: (1) a reward for staying where the command started, in every stance skill, (2) a reward for the height of the foot that should be lifted in the flamingo, whether or not it still touches the ground.

rs_v7 goes from 0.087 to about 0.097–0.10 (smoothed), a smaller gain than rs_v6's 0.055 → 0.084, because lift was already 83–97 % of the call. The new skill_spot term sits at 0.43–0.45 of 1.5 for the whole run without trending, yet the drill drift falls from 0.17–0.90 m to 0.06–0.14 m at 1499. The path was not monotonic: 0.21–0.33 m at 400, 0.11–0.23 at 800, back up to 0.14–0.47 at 1200 (1 of 10) as the lift overshot, then down again. A checkpoint between gates could be worse than either neighbour, so the gate result, not the curve, picks the brain.

🎒 Child

This score is high when one foot stays in the air and the other foot stands still. Both lines are flat and jumpy. We gave the robot extra points for lifting its foot, but it still puts the foot down again and again and hops sideways.

🎓 Adult

Flat at about 0.064–0.068 through rs_v7 (rs_v6: 0.077 → 0.068). The new skill_flamingo_lift term stays at 0.17 of 1.5 and the slip penalty at −0.05, neither moving. In the drill all ten trials fail at every gate: 120–135 touch ticks and 11–20 cm slip at 400–1200, 160+ ticks and 25 cm at 1499. The skill existed at rs_v6 iteration 2550 (10/10, 0 touches) and was lost while march was learned; rs_v7 started from the checkpoint that had already lost it and did not recover it. A flamingo-capable shared brain has to start from 2550 or be trained without the trade-off.

3 · Turning accuracy is still eroding

Episode_Reward/track_ang (how closely the commanded turn rate is followed; paid in every mode)

🎒 Child

This score is high when the robot turns exactly as fast as it is told. It went down during the blue try and it keeps going down in the yellow try. The robot is getting better at its new tricks and slowly worse at walking and turning.

🎓 Adult

0.98 → 0.83 over rs_v6 and 0.83 → 0.71 over rs_v7; the Rung 2 gate sits at 5–7 of 10 with no falls. The policy's action noise rises through both runs (std 0.44 → 0.50 → 0.54) even with the entropy coefficient at 0.0025. GRANDMA's Rung 2 runs tonight showed the same pattern, and there lowering the coefficient to 0.001 with a capped starting std stopped the noise growth and the reward decline. That makes entropy-driven noise the leading suspect for this erosion; it has not been tested on this task. rs_v8 will test it: same task, coefficient 0.001, starting std capped at 0.3.
