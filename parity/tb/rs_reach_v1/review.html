MATT arm-reach training review: rs_reach_v1

2026-10-02 04:04, iteration 1199 · MATT (mattbio body) · 1200 iterations in 67 min · rs_v8 = the stance brain it started from (orange, the flat lower line) and rs_reach_v1 = this run (orange, the rising line: TensorBoard gave both the same colour) rs_reach_v2 = a second try, stopped after 18 min (dark)

Verdict: reaches most targets, but not six in a row

In one sentence: the hand now lands within 8 cm of 22 of 24 single targets (8 of 24 before the run), but the test for Event 4 needs six targets in a row and passed on only 1 of 10 tries.

What is left: targets further away than the straight arm can reach (61 cm from the shoulder; targets go out to 71 cm). Those need the body to lean, and it does not lean: the hand ends 10 to 30 cm short, low and towards the arm's own side. Almost every six-target chain contains one such target.

What was changed for this run: half of the practice commands are reaches (13 % before), an extra reward step between "near" and "on target", and no penalty for leaning the trunk while reaching.

The second try (dark line) drew half of its reaches from the far zone. After 300 iterations the far targets had moved 1 to 2 cm, so it was stopped: at that pace they would not get inside the bar in one night.

Brain: rs_reach_v1_it1199.onnx is the best reach brain so far; Event 4 is not built and this brain does not pass its test. Open question for the user: shorten the commanded reach (it asks for 10 cm more than the arm has), or keep it and train the lean.

🎓 Adult

The first jump (0.05 to 0.25 in 50 iterations) is mostly the command mix: reach went from 13 % to 50 % of commands, a factor of 3.8. The learning is the climb after it, 0.25 to 0.40, which matches the CPU probe (median error 9.5 to 3.1 cm). rs_reach_v2 starts from v1's final brain but reads 0.35: half of its targets are in the 80-95 % zone where v1 is weakest, and it is flat over its 326 iterations.

🎒 Child

This one only pays when the hand is almost exactly on the target. It rose in a straight line for the whole run and had not flattened at the end, so the brain was still getting more precise on the targets it can reach.

🎓 Adult

Near-linear growth to 0.076 with no plateau: precision on reachable targets is still improving at iteration 1199. The drill, though, is decided by the worst target of six, and those are the far ones, where this term pays nothing (errors of 10-30 cm against a 3 cm kernel). More of the same training would raise this curve without changing the drill result.
